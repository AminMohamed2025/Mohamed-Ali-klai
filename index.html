Muhammad Ali

Muhammad Ali (born Cassius Marcellus Clay Jr.; Jan 17, 1942 – Jun 3, 2016) — "The Greatest"

About Muhammad Ali

🥊 Muhammad Ali was an American professional boxer and activist. Nicknamed "The Greatest", he is regarded as one of the most significant sports figures of the 20th century.

🥊 Frequently ranked as the greatest heavyweight boxer of all time, Ali was involved in several historic boxing matches and feuds, most notably his fights with Joe Frazier and George Foreman.

🥊 In 1999, he was named Sportsman of the Century by Sports Illustrated and the Sports Personality of the Century by the BBC.
