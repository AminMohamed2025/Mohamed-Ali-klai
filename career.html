Career Highlights

Major Milestones

  • 1960 Olympic Gold Medal: Won the light heavyweight gold medal at the Summer Olympics in Rome at age 18.
  • 1964 World Champion: Defeated Sonny Liston in a major upset to win the world heavyweight title.
  • 1971 Fight of the Century: Faced Joe Frazier in one of the most famous bouts in boxing history.
  • 1974 Rumble in the Jungle: Defeated George Foreman in Kinshasa, Zaire, using his famous "Rope-a-Dope" strategy.
  • 1975 Thrilla in Manila: Final victory over Joe Frazier in the Philippines.
